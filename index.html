What happens to a resume

  1. CleanLinks, emails and numbers are stripped. Empty and duplicate resumes never reach the model.
  2. VectorizeTF-IDF turns words and word pairs into numbers. The first line, usually the job title, gets its own weight.
  3. ClassifyA linear SVM picks one of 24 fields. It was chosen over two rivals by 5-fold cross-validation.
  4. WriteGemini drafts the resume using only the facts you give it, guided by the keywords of the detected field.
