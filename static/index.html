How it works

Review me samjhane ke liye.

Pipeline

  1. Dataset: 2484 resumes, 24 job categories (CSV).
  2. Cleaning: lowercase, URL/email/numbers hataye, duplicates aur khaali resumes nikaale.
  3. Split: 80% training, 20% testing (stratified, har category dono me).
  4. Features: TF-IDF (1-2 word combos) resume body par + resume ki pehli line (job title) par alag.
  5. Models compared: LinearSVC, Logistic Regression, Complement Naive Bayes (5-fold cross-validation).
  6. Best model CV score se choose hota hai, phir test set par final accuracy nikalti hai.
  7. Backend: FastAPI endpoints /api/train, /api/predict, /api/generate.
  8. Generative AI: Gemini LLM, classifier ke domain ko prompt me leke resume likhta hai (sirf diye gaye facts, kuch invent nahi karta).

Generative AI kahan hai?

  • Classifier = discriminative model (category predict karta hai).
  • Resume writing = generative LLM (naya text banata hai).
  • Dono mil ke: pehle domain pehchano, phir us domain ke keywords ke saath resume generate karo.

Limitations (honest answer)

  • BPO aur Automobile jaisi categories me data kam hai, isliye wahan accuracy kam hai.
  • Resume ki pehli line job title honi chahiye, tab best result aata hai.
  • AI sirf wahi likhta hai jo aap dete ho. Facts aap ko khud verify karne chahiye.
